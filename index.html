Outreach

Outreach is a private, self-hosted tool. Its owner uses it to write and schedule personal emails and follow-ups, sent from their own Gmail account through the official Gmail API.

It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and it is used only by its owner with the Gmail accounts they connect themselves.

Read the privacy policy to see what Google data Outreach uses and how.
